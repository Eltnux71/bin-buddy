# Bin Buddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Bin Buddy is designed to help users view local bin collection schedules and receive reminders about upcoming collections.

## Information We Collect

Bin Buddy does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

The app does not require users to create an account and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment information.

## Notifications

Bin Buddy may request permission to send notifications. These notifications are used solely to remind users about upcoming bin collections.

Notification preferences are stored locally on the user's device.

## Network Requests

The app retrieves bin collection information from publicly available local authority data sources. Requests are made solely to obtain collection schedules and are not used to track users.

## Data Storage

Bin Buddy stores only the information required for the app to function, such as collection schedules and notification settings. This information is stored locally on the user's device.

## Third-Party Services

Bin Buddy does not sell user data and does not share personal information with third parties.

The app may rely on platform services provided by Google Android and Expo to deliver notifications and operate correctly.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published alongside future app releases.
